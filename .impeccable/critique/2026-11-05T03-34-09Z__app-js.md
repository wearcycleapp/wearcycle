## Design Specificity Verdict

## Overall Impression

Both work, in all seven languages, with two clipped lines. The biggest opportunity is the image: it is the one piece of Wearcycle that leaves the app and reaches people who have never seen it.

## What's Working

- The image is drawn entirely on the device, fixed at 1080x1920, in the person's language and date format.
- The privacy notice is specific about who processes what and where, in all seven languages.
- The manifest and theme-color metas already use palette values: #f6f4f1 and #12161e equal --bg in light and dark; #1b2433 is ink.

## Priority Issues

- [P1] Text cut off in the image. "Deux couleurs d'accent qui se font concurrence (bourgogne + vert)" runs 30px past the edge on French Chores, and the Tagalog line 3px: 6 of 126 images (one line, never wrapped).
- [P1] The headline sits under social apps' controls. The highest text starts at 108px; third-party guides to Meta's Stories safe zone give about the top 14% (269px of 1920).
- [P1] Soft pictures. A piece without a cut-out draws its 256px thumbnail: with no cut-outs 56 of 273 pictures are drawn larger than 1.1x their file, up to 1.6x, although a 1024px copy has been stored since v1.62.1. SCREENS.md and CLAUDE.md pick images by drawn size.
- [P1] privacy.html ignores the app's Light or Dark choice and the app's fonts (0 of 2 choices followed; What's new follows both).
- [P2] Not the chosen direction: the image uses a flat-lay board with no names; phones (direction A) name every piece.
- [P2] Colors outside tokens: the #e5dccf gradient stop; the white swatch's ring is canvas-colored, so white reads 1.1:1. The image's fonts are not waited for (wishCard does).
- [P2] privacy.html: language links 34px, no theme-color metas (What's new has none either), no link back at the top.

## Persona Red Flags

- A friend receiving the image: sees clothes with no names, and the headline may sit under Instagram's top bar.
- A French-speaking user sharing Chores: the palette sentence is cut off.
- A user who set the app to Dark on a light phone: the privacy page opens bright cream.
- A keyboard user on the privacy page: reaches a link back only after every link on the page.

## Minor Observations

- The manifest's ink splash cannot follow Light or Dark: a dark override exists only as a WICG proposal (user_preferences).
- wishCard draws with the same values and needs no change.

## Questions to Consider

- Should the image name the pieces, as phones do? The names are what the person typed, and they would leave the app with the image.
