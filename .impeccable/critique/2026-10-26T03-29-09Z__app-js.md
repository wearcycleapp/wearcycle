## Design specificity verdict

LLM assessment: the flat-lay board on the cream canvas is specific to Wearcycle and is the strongest part of the screen: it reads as a styled outfit, not a list of records. Around it, the screen is generic app scaffolding: two rows of pill chips, a card with a rank badge, a dots pager, a collapsed accordion, a carousel of cards. Nothing in that scaffolding is wrong, but it pushes the outfit down and the decision ("Wear this") off the first screen, and it does not change with the device: the iPad portrait layout is the phone layout scaled up (a 676px board at 820px), and from 960px it becomes v1.41's hero beside a grid. The chosen directions (A Kit on phones, B Lookbook from 700x600) answer exactly these points.

Deterministic scan (npx impeccable@4.5.2 detect, before run): 2 warnings, both side-tab, both Outfits CSS: `.askcard` (styles.css:612, the laundry and dress-code cards, 3px coral left border) and the unused `.advice` (styles.css:354). Advisories: 1 repeating-stripes-gradient in the Outfits snapshot, from `.flatlay`'s v1.9 striped background that a later rule always overrides (dead code that the detector still sees), and 79 design-system advisories (font sizes, radii, colors) in styles.css lines 196 to 760, the Outfits rules. axe-core: 4 serious target-size nodes on every Outfits view (the pager dots), 64 of the run's 144 serious nodes. No contrast finding. Agreement: the side stripe and the dead stripes gradient are real; the LLM review and the detector agree that the selection colors break DESIGN.md.

Visual overlays: not run (no live browser presentation in this cloud session; the detector ran on the check's saved snapshots).

## Overall impression

The outfit itself looks good; the screen around it does not let you act on it. Biggest opportunity: make "Wear this" part of the first screen at every size, which the two chosen compositions do by moving everything that is not the outfit out of the way between the picture and the button.

## What's working

- The flat-lay: cut-outs on the warm canvas, consistent cell sizes, wearing order (upper body left, trousers and shoes right). It is the product's identity and stays as B's board.
- Explanations exist and are honest: a match label tied to the score, a note count for warnings, the ranking rule written out.
- Recovery is everywhere: Back to suggestion, leave a piece out, New ideas, Correct after logging.

## Priority issues

- **[P1] "Wear this" is below the first screen at 12 of the 13 sizes in SCREENS.md.** 950 to 998px at 390x844, 923 to 971 at 360x800, 1352 to 1400 at 820x1180, 761 to 809 at 1366x630, 1061 to 1109 at 1920x1080; only 1024x1366 shows it. The same on the real account: 1131 to 1179 at 390, 845 to 893 at 1366x768, 1528 to 1576 in WebKit at 820 (live check run 40263041143). At 390x844 the board starts at 317px (header, day row, both chip rows and the card header come first), and four elements sit between the board and the button (cut-out note, tap hint, pager, palette). Fix: direction A on phones (all six occasions in a 3x2 grid, style as a menu, the kit, Wear this right under it); direction B from 700x600 (text tabs, a square board sized by viewport height). Command: /impeccable layout.
- **[P1] Pictures drawn larger than their files, and the same file downloaded many times.** At 390x844 (pixel ratio 3) 3 of the 5 first-screen pictures are drawn up to 1.7x larger than their file (2x at 820, 1.9x on a phone turned sideways); on the real account 3 of 3 up to 1.2x at 390 and 1.7x in WebKit at 820. On first open the fixture downloads 87 files (478 KB) for Outfits, with the same cut-out fetched up to 20 times: cutUrl (app.js:373) caches the result only after the download ends, and each render of the screen starts a new download. Fix: pick each source by drawn size x pixel ratio; share one download per cut-out. Command: /impeccable optimize.
- **[P1] The option pager is too small to tap.** Dots are 8x8px buttons (axe target-size, serious, every Outfits view), arrows 36x32px. Fix: in B, 24px dot buttons with a visible 8px mark, 44px arrows and "1 of 4"; in A, options as a list of rows at least 56px tall. Command: /impeccable harden.
- **[P2] Selection colors and hidden choices.** Selected option = coral pill, selected holiday chip = coral fill (DESIGN.md: selected states in ink). At 390px the occasion row shows Work to Chores (cut) and hides Formal; the style row hides Minimal; both scroll with no scrollbar. Fix: ink fill (A) or a 2px ink underline (B) with aria-pressed; all six occasions visible in A. Command: /impeccable clarify.
- **[P2] The outfit is not named and the options are anonymous.** The board shows no names (they are in aria-labels only); options show a match label and a mini board, no pieces; on phones the options scroll sideways with the 4th cut off. Focus order on the board follows flCells (top, accessories, trousers, shoes), not the reading order. Fix: A's kit names every piece; A's options become a numbered index with piece names; B's pages keep names in the page and Why section; Tab order follows the visual order. Command: /impeccable layout.
