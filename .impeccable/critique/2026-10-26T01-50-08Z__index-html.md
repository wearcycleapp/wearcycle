## Design specificity verdict

LLM assessment: the shell is quiet and correct for the brand (ink, raised bar, coral only on the active icon) but generic in structure: a stock four-tab bar that becomes a stock icon rail at 960px. It is not wrong to be conventional here (Operate mode: platform convention outranks expression); the specificity should come from the screens. What is missing is adaptation: the shell does not change between an iPad held upright and a phone, or between a phone held upright and turned sideways, and the 1920px layout leaves the rail stranded at the edge.

Deterministic scan (npx impeccable@4.5.2 detect, from the before run): 11 warnings. 9 of them are the Add clothes button's shadow (dark-glow: rgba(20,30,60,.25) is a colored shadow on the dark theme; 2 in index.html, 7 in the screen snapshots). The other 2 are side-tab stripes on .askcard and the unused .advice (Outfits, Phase 8). Advisories (101) are not shell-specific. Agreement: the dark FAB shadow contradicts DESIGN.md ("dark mode conveys depth with contrast and borders. No glows"). No false positives in the shell.

Visual overlays: not run (no live browser presentation in this cloud session; the detector ran on saved snapshots).

## Overall impression

Solid on a phone held upright, unfinished everywhere else. Biggest opportunity: one condition for the whole app (700px wide and 600px tall) so the shell and the screen direction switch together, with Settings in the rail.

## What's working

- One clear current-tab state, with aria-current="page" and the coral icon used exactly where DESIGN.md allows it.
- Labels under every icon, already in seven languages; no truncation at 360px.
- Fixed layers (toasts, job bar, Add clothes) already account for the bottom inset.

## Priority issues

- **[P1] Header covers the day row when a top inset exists.** header.top is position:relative with top:env(safe-area-inset-top) on top of :root's padding-top with the same inset. With a 47px inset (emulated) the header is drawn at 94px instead of 47px and covers 29px of the "Wednesday / weather" row. Fix: drop the relative top offset (or make the header sticky with that offset). Command: /impeccable harden.
- **[P1] Keyboard focus and order.** The nav is the last element in the DOM, so it is reached last (38 Tab presses at 1366px), and on Closet "Add clothes" is 42 presses away. At 390x844, 6 focus stops on Outfits (option arrows and dots) end up entirely behind the bottom bar (7 at 844x390): fails WCAG 2.2 SC 2.4.11. Fix: header, navigation and Add clothes first in the DOM; scroll-padding-bottom equal to the bar. Command: /impeccable harden.
- **[P1] 700 to 1279px and 1920px.** iPad portrait (744 to 834px) gets the phone bar under a 760px column; Settings is not in the rail; at 1920px the rail is 346px from the content. Fix: the rail from 700px wide and 600px tall with Settings at its bottom; a 208px labelled rail with the wordmark from 1280px. Command: /impeccable adapt.
- **[P2] Phones turned sideways.** The bar is 66px plus a 21px bottom inset of a 390px screen (22%); the Add clothes button sits inside the right safe area (16px from the edge, inset 47px). Fix: a lower bar with icon beside label (both Android and iOS do this in landscape); side insets on every fixed layer. Command: /impeccable adapt.
- **[P2] Dark theme and small type.** The Care badge is white on #f2668b, 2.98:1; the Add clothes shadow is a colored glow in dark; the Settings button is 36px (rule 44px); tab labels 11.5px, badge 10px, status 11px (rule 12px minimum). Command: /impeccable polish.

## Persona red flags

- **Sam (keyboard, screen reader):** the sections navigation comes after the whole screen; focus disappears behind the bar on Outfits; no hover or press feedback on the tabs.
- **Casey (one hand, phone):** Settings only at the top of the screen, and the header scrolls away; turned sideways, the bar and header leave about 244px for content.
- **Alex (Windows, mouse):** at 1366px in French the Closet category row is 17px too wide with its scrollbar hidden, so "Autre accessoire" cannot be reached with a mouse wheel; no hover on any shell control.

## Minor observations

- .version sets a mono font and letter spacing that the typography rule always overrides (2 dead declarations).
- 100vh in three Outfits rules and 56vh in the piece preview: wrong height on mobile Safari when the toolbar shows.
- No -webkit-text-size-adjust: iOS Safari enlarges text when a phone turns sideways.
- The Care badge is positioned for a centered icon; in a labelled row it would sit on the icon.

## Questions to consider

- Should the header stay on screen while scrolling, now that Settings moves to the rail on wide screens?
- On a phone turned sideways, is height (bar) or width (rail) the scarcer resource?
- Should hover feedback stop at the shell, or reach every button and chip now?
