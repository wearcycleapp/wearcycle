## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM assessment: the camera and the viewer are what they should be, functional black layers, and the tips screen is specific to the job (light, background, whole piece, one per photo). The sign-in page is not. It is the first thing a new public user sees, and it could belong to any product: logo and wordmark, a 40px headline, a grey paragraph, three bullet points with coral dots, then a white card with a soft shadow holding a pill switch and a form. The product's signature (garments laid out on the cream canvas, ink structure, the condensed display face used with intent) appears only in the headline. Nothing on the page shows a piece of clothing, although PRODUCT.md says clothes lead every screen and DESIGN.md calls the cream board the signature. On a computer the page is the phone layout centred in the window: 420px of 1366 (31%) and of 1920 (22%), which DESIGN.md lists as a don't. What is this? and the consent sheet share the Phase 12 sheet frame, which already works; their faults are local (unnamed colours, an explanation below the buttons).

Deterministic scan: the detector found 1 warning across 10 layer snapshots: low-contrast in the viewer, "text #ffffff on #fbfaf8, 1.0:1" (the Photo and Cut-out labels). index.html and styles.css: 0 warnings, 107 advisories (design-system advisories, as in the baseline). axe-core: 2 serious nodes, color-contrast on the same two viewer buttons; moderate on the sign-in page: landmark-one-main (no main landmark) and region (10 nodes outside a landmark). The detector and axe agree with the review on the viewer switch; neither can see the layering, focus or truncation problems below, which the probe measured. No false positives.

## Overall Impression

The layers work in the happy path, and the camera's tips and Done button (v1.58.2) are good. The problems sit at the edges: messages hidden under the camera, translated progress cut off, a switch you cannot read, two dialogs a keyboard cannot use. The biggest opportunity is the sign-in page: it is the brand moment for every new user and today it shows a form and no clothes.

## What's Working

- The many-photos camera: a "Before you start" screen with four concrete tips, a labelled "✓ Done (N)" that turns white once there is a photo, and a spoken status line after each photo ("Photo 3 saved. Next piece, or tap Done to add them.").
- Honest consent: the sign-up checkbox and the consent sheet name who receives the data and where, offer "Agree, but turn AI features off" and say what that costs (about 20 seconds per piece).
- Sign-in fields are 16px and 44px tall (Phase 12), passwords have Show, new passwords are typed twice, and the keyboard order is logical (mode, email, passwords, consent, button, language).

## Priority Issues

- [P1] Messages and labels the person cannot read. Toasts (z-index 30) open under the camera (40) and the viewer (60): "That photo was dark, so it was brightened…", "Looking for the camera with the light…" and "This phone does not let web apps use its light…" are never seen (elementFromPoint at the toast's centre returns the camera on all 6 devices tested). The job bar cuts its text with an ellipsis: French "Ajout de 2 sur 5 · Lecture de la ph…" (210 of 215px) and Tagalog (210 of 257px) at 360; CLAUDE.md: never truncate translated labels. The viewer's Photo / Cut-out switch is white text on #fbfaf8, 1.04:1 (detector and axe). Why: the dark-photo advice exists to get better photos; progress is the only sign that a long job is running. Fix: toasts above every layer and above the camera's bar while it is open; the job text gets the full width (progress as a thin line under it) and wraps rather than cutting; the viewer switch drawn for a dark layer, the chosen mode in white fill. Command: harden.
- [P1] The sign-in page has no garment and no composition beyond a phone column. Measured: a new user at 360x800 sees the brand, a headline, a paragraph, three dotted perks and the top of a white card; at 1366x768 and 1920x1080 a 420px column in an empty window; on an iPad in landscape (1180x820) and at 1366x768 the Create account button is below the window. Coral marks the perk bullets (DESIGN.md: coral is the action signal only) and the form card has the soft shadow (Flat-By-Default). The language choice is a select at the very end of the page. Why: it is the first impression for every new public user and the one place the tagline appears. Fix: a flat-lay on the cream canvas built from the app's own garment glyphs (the pictures a new user's pieces get before a photo), the headline at display size, the perks with ink marks, the form flat on the page; from 760px wide the brand and the board on the left and the form on the right; the language button in the brand row, as in the app's header. Commands: layout, typeset.
- [P1] The camera and the zoom viewer cannot be used with a keyboard. Neither moves focus into itself, neither marks the page behind inert or itself aria-modal; 6 of 6 Tab presses land behind the viewer, 4 of 6 behind the camera (the first stops on the dialog, then the header and tabs). Escape works on both. Why: Windows with a keyboard is a supported platform and Phase 10 made every picture open the viewer from the keyboard, so a keyboard user can open it but not use it. Fix: the Phase 12 sheet pattern: focus the close button (viewer) or the first action (camera), the app and any sheet behind inert, focus back to the opener on close. Command: harden.
- [P2] Status, controls and states outside the system. Sign-in: confirmations ("Almost there: open the confirmation email…", "If that email has an account, a reset link is on its way.") use the error red; errors are not in a live region; the Create account / Sign in switch is 40px, "Forgot password?" and "Back to sign in" 36px; no main landmark (axe). Camera: Tips, flashlight and switch camera 36px tall. Viewer: close, zoom out and zoom in 36px, Fit 38x40, the switch 40px; the close is a text ✕ while sheets use the drawn icon. Toasts and the job bar create their role=status element together with the message, which screen readers may not announce (a live region should be in the page before it changes; htmhell.dev, "ARIA Live Regions", 2023). A sticky failure toast covers the Add clothes button on phones (390: toast 640-760, button 716-768; the button's centre hits the toast), and any toast does while a job runs. Fix: a status style for confirmations, persistent live regions, 44px controls, the toast above Add clothes. Commands: harden, polish.
- [P2] Smaller faults in the two sheets and the camera. What is this?: the piece is an 88px picture beside the name, and the 19 colour swatches have no visible names, so navy, black, denim and blue are told apart by colour alone. The consent sheet puts "Without AI features, you set each piece's category…" after the buttons, where the sticky bar overlaps its first line by 6px and at 360x640 it is below the sheet. Camera: Cancel after photos keeps them (acts like Done); in a phone turned sideways the bottom bar and top bar leave the video 250 of 390px. Fix: a larger picture (the garment sheet's layout from 700px), the chosen colours named under the swatches, the consequence above the buttons; Cancel asks before discarding; the camera's controls on the side in landscape. Commands: clarify, adapt.

## Minor Observations

- The viewer's background (#0b0d12), loading text (#aaa) and note (#c9ccd3) are hard-coded; they pass contrast (8.37:1 and 12.09:1).
- The job bar's coral progress is 1.92:1 against its track in light mode (2.57:1 against the bar); the text says "2 of 5", so the bar is not the only cue.
- The camera tips use Unicode characters as icons (☀ ▭ ⤢), while the rest of the app draws SVG.
- The sign-in language select keeps "Language" in English as its accessible name in every language (data-notr).
- The manifest's splash colour is ink (#1b2433) while the light theme opens on cream; a flash on Android when the app starts (not verified on a device).
- On phones turned sideways, the camera's bar and top bar have no side safe-area padding, so Cancel can sit under a notch.
- Closet's In the wash list (out of this phase except its picture) shows raw dates ("last on 2026-10-09") and keeps the shadowed panel.
- The Outfits pin card has a coral border (decision 1: coral is not a state colour).
