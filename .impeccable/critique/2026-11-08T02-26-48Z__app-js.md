## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: authored for this product. The cream garment canvas behind every piece, the numbered picks (01 to 04) with match bars, the Big Shoulders day and occasion headings and the Ink & Coral palette could not be moved to another app unchanged. Care's three groups (Repair, Let go, Check, Rotate) and Shop's "most new pairings" use the product's own logic as content rather than generic cards. The most interchangeable parts are the lists inside Care and Shop (small uppercase label, row, ghost buttons), which read like a settings list, and the "Why this outfit?" bullets.

**Deterministic scan** (npx impeccable@4.5.2 detect, run by tools/check.mjs on index.html, styles.css and 54 screen snapshots): 0 warnings on the main screens (Phase 4 baseline 15: dark-glow 9, low-contrast 4, side-tab 2); 11 warnings on added views, all cramped-padding, reviewed one by one:
- 9 sheets (Adjust, Condition check, Diary, Help, Inspiration, Inspiration result, Language, Reminders, What I wore): ".sheet children flush on bottom". False positive: the sheet's bottom padding is calc(20px + env(safe-area-inset-bottom,0px)); with a plain 20px the same 4 snapshots give no sheet warning (detector run on copies), so the detector reads the calc with env() as 0.
- Language list (.langlist) and the wish list's Measurements box (.wlmeas): rows touch the box's border. False positive in effect: each child is a 48px row with its text centred (14px inset), the usual grouped-list pattern; screenshots at 390 look right.
- 110 advisories (Phase 4: 149): design-system-font-size 82 (Phase 4: 65), radius 16, color 12; the snapshot advisories went from 54 to 0. The font-size count rose with the new screens: the type-scale drift above.

**Visual overlays**: not attempted. The browser overlay needs Impeccable's live server, which inserts a localhost script into index.html (CLAUDE.md: only with care); the snapshot scan covers the same rules.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Opening Care froze the page for 2.2 s on a slow phone.** Why it matters: taps do nothing while "Not getting picked" is ranked (outfits for every occasion). Fix: rank in a background worker with the same logic.js (built this phase, owner's high finding). Suggested command: /impeccable optimize.
- **[P1] Banners above the outfit on phones.** The install prompt pushed Wear this under the tab bar (767 to 815 of 844 at 390). Offline, the header's Settings button runs off the right edge at 360 and 390 (the OFFLINE pill takes its room; last pixel at 359 of 360, normally 343). The language suggestion squeezes its sentence into a 4-line column at 390. Fix: install prompt off Outfits (built, owner's high finding); on phones hide the header pill while the offline banner says the same; stack the suggestion's buttons under its sentence. Suggested command: /impeccable adapt.
- **[P1] Share pictures: text 10 to 11px on a phone, words broken, names in grey.** Kinds 30 and 34px, small names 32px of 1080; "Chrono- / graph"; shoes and accessories in muted grey; wish-list labels 30px. Fix (owner, 2026-11-07): every text 36px or more (12px seen 360px wide), whole words, all names in ink, room left over used for larger accessories. Suggested command: /impeccable typeset.
- **[P2] No type scale.** 25 font sizes in styles.css, including 9px (the header's version line at 460px and under), 10, 11 and 11.5px (Example tag, AI label, segment captions, calendar weekdays). Fix: a 6-step scale as tokens and a 12px floor, the rule the owner set for the share picture. Suggested command: /impeccable typeset.
- **[P2] Suggestions change on their own in big closets.** suggest() in logic.js ranks a random sample of 3,000 combinations when an occasion has more (about 100 pieces); the same closet can give a different pick or a different Care list each time it is worked out (it is also why the 200-piece test page differs between runs). Fix: a seeded sample (same closet and day, same result). Needs the owner's approval (logic.js). Suggested command: none (logic).
