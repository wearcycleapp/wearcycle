## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM assessment: Settings is the generic mobile settings card stack: white rounded panels with a soft shadow on a cream sheet, a bold 14px title with a muted 12.5px line, a button or a checkbox at the right. It could belong to any product; the only Wearcycle traits are the display-face sheet title and the ink chips. That is acceptable for QUIET UTILITY in spirit (conventional), but the execution is noisier than conventional: settings apps group rows on one surface with hairlines; here every group is a separate floating card, and the Laundry and Reminders blocks drop the cards entirely, so the sheet changes vocabulary half way down. The other sheets share the frame and its faults (36px close button, no focus, 15px fields) and add their own: stat cards (Closet stats), coral selection (Your style, Style palette, What I wore picks), a light beige picture box in dark mode (Your style).

## Overall Impression

Every setting is there and explained, and nothing is broken in the happy path. The two biggest problems are not visual: a keyboard or screen-reader user cannot work inside any sheet, and every text field zooms the page on an iPhone or iPad. Visually, Settings is a long stack of separate cards where one grouped list would do, and on a computer it is a 620px box in a 1366px window that needs 4.5 screens of scrolling.

## What's Working

- Each setting has a one-line explanation in plain words, translated into six languages (only one line is left in English).
- Destructive and outward actions are guarded: Delete my data needs DELETE typed; Share my list requires sizes; the AI switch names who receives the data and where.
- Sheets centre from 700px wide and keep a sticky action row (Save, Cancel) where there is one.

## Priority Issues

- [P1] Sheets do not work with a keyboard or a screen reader. Measured at 1366x768 (Settings, Language, Delete my data, Add clothes): on open, focus stays on the page (body or the rail button); three Tab presses all land behind the dialog; the dialog (role=dialog, aria-modal) has no accessible name. Escape closes it and focus ends on a button behind it. All 29 sheet openers share openSheet, so this is one fix. Why: Windows with a keyboard is a supported platform (CLAUDE.md); aria-modal tells assistive tech the page behind is inert while focus is there. Fix: openSheet names the dialog by its heading (aria-labelledby), moves focus into the sheet, keeps Tab and Shift+Tab inside, and returns focus to the control that opened it (as Phase 10 did for the zoom viewer). Command: harden.
- [P1] Every form field zooms the page on iPhone and iPad Safari. All measured fields are 15px: Change password 3 of 3, Choose a new password 2 of 2, Weather 2 of 2 (postal code, country), Delete my data, What is this? (name), Share my list 10 of 10 size boxes, the Language select in Settings. iOS Safari zooms into a focused field under 16px (squirrelscan rule mobile/input-zoom; the mobile-native skill's "Page zooms into input" fix). Fix: 16px for input, select and textarea; fields 44px tall (Share my list sizes are 40px). DESIGN.md keeps body text at 15px; fields become the stated exception. Command: adapt.
- [P1] Settings is a card stack, not a grouped list. 12 white panels with the soft shadow inside the sheet (DESIGN.md: no cards inside cards, flat by default), then the Laundry and Reminders steppers flat with their own dividers. 4.85 screens at 390, 5.97 in French at 360, 5.42 in Japanese, 2.96 at 820, 4.55 at 1366 (sheet 620x676). Each of the four steppers takes a 48px stepper, a 26px uppercase display value and four presets: about one screen for Reminders alone. Fix: one surface, group headings, aligned rows (title, explanation, control at the right) divided by hairlines; steppers as one row (label, value, minus and plus) with the presets as small chips under it; on wide screens a wider sheet with an index of the groups. Commands: distill, layout.
- [P2] Controls outside the system. The close button is 36px on all 27 sheets (rule 44px); most sheet buttons are 40 or 42px; the colour buttons in What is this? are 34px and the diary arrows 36px. Checkboxes in Settings and Delete my data draw the browser's default blue (no accent-color; Share my list already uses ink). Selected states in coral: Style palette (coral border only, no second cue: decision 1), Your style cards (coral border and coral inset ring), What I wore picks (coral border with a soft fill). Row titles are one line with an ellipsis, which cuts the email and the French "Recadrer les photos sur les vêtements". Fix: 44px close and buttons (40px small chips), ink accent-color, ink selected states with a check mark or fill, titles that wrap. Command: polish.
- [P2] Pictures and data sheets. Your style draws its pictures on #ece8e2 in both themes (a light beige box in dark mode; SCREENS.md known issue). Closet stats is the hero-metric template (three shadowed stat cards) and its rows use thumbBox: the floor photo even for pieces with a cut-out (Navy chinos has one), a zoom badge over the corner, and a role=button div without tabindex (not reachable by keyboard, the fault Phase 10 fixed in Care). What's new uses system fonts, ignores the app's Light/Dark choice (app set to Dark on a light phone: light page) and has 36px language links. Fix: the cream canvas tokens for the style pictures; numbers as type, not cards; the Care picture (cut-out first, keyboard reachable); What's new with the app's fonts and theme. Commands: quieter, harden.
