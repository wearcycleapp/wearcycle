## Design Specificity Verdict

Specific in content (the person's own pieces, +N outfits, condition labels, occasions), generic in structure: a stack of admin panels. The screen answers "what should I buy" in its first line, then keeps answering it five more ways.

## Cognitive Load

- Decision points with more than 4 visible options: the screen as a whole (6 panels; 11 rows that open; 3 or more Shop buttons; Share my list; Choose and From a photo; Suggest).
- Checklist failures: no single place lists everything worth buying; overlapping lists (by need, by occasion, by season, by style, by Claude); a section's purpose is only clear after reading its rows.

## Emotional Journey

The opening is calm and honest ("Couvert" in green). The middle is a valley: a long scroll where the same gaps return under new headings, which reads as "you need more" (PRODUCT.md: never encourage buying to fill a screen). The end is a button to ask for even more ideas.

## Strengths

1. One answer first, then evidence; covered occasions said plainly.
2. The person's own pieces as pictures on Replace cards; +N outfits in the display face.
3. Accessible rows (aria-expanded, 44px), the open state kept for the session.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Too many lists at once** (owner's report). Six panels, five list styles, 2.9 screens closed on a French phone. Fix: one short screen of sections; each opens its details.
2. **[P1] Overlapping needs.** A gap shows in À acheter and again in Par occasion; season, style and Claude ideas are separate "to add" lists. Fix: each section says what it is for in one line and how many things are in it; the first section stays the answer.
3. **[P2] "Achats" means two things in French.** The tab and the button that searches the web share the word. Fix: a French translation that names the action (for example "Magasiner"), no English change.
4. **[P2] Department question first.** When the department is not set, its card sits above the answer. Fix: put it under the summary or in the first section's details.
5. **[P3] Desktop right column** is mostly empty while the left runs to 1,447px at 1366x768.

## Persona Red Flags

- New person, small closet: sees the most "to add" badges, the most lists, with the least context.
- Owner, French, phone: the reported problem; the screen reads as a wall of lists.
