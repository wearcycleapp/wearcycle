Privacy at Wearcycle

Last updated October 4, 2026. Wearcycle is a personal wardrobe app. This page says what it stores, where, and who processes it.

What is stored

  • Your account: email address and a hashed password, managed by Supabase Auth.
  • Your clothes: photos, cut-outs and the details you or Claude fill in (name, category, colours, condition, price paid if you add it).
  • Your outfit log and settings: what you wore and when, reminders, style palette, and, if you turn on weather, an approximate location rounded to about 1 km (or the first three characters of a Canadian postal code).

Everything is kept in a Supabase database and private storage bucket protected by row-level security, so only your signed-in account can read your rows and files.

Who processes your data

  • Anthropic (Claude): when you add a photo, ask for a condition check or shopping ideas, that photo or a text summary of your closet is sent to Anthropic's API to fill in details. Anthropic states that, by default, it does not use inputs or outputs from its commercial products, including the API, to train its models.
  • Open-Meteo receives rounded coordinates to return the forecast; Zippopotam.us receives the postal code you type to find its area. Both only if you turn on weather.
  • Background removal for flat-lay cut-outs runs on your phone; those photos are not sent anywhere for that step.

What Wearcycle does not do

No ads, no selling or sharing of your data, no tracking analytics.

Your choices

  • Delete my data in Settings removes your clothes, photos, cut-outs, outfit log and settings from the server.
  • To close the sign-in account itself, email wearcycle.app@gmail.com from your account address.
